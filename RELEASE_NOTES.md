# UniversalSaveEditor v0.13.0-alpha

Keep your favorite games close and move between the library and editor with subtle transitions, in both light and dark appearances.

- Use the **star** on a cover to add or remove a favorite. Favorites stay at the beginning of the library, with your chosen sort order within each group.
- Open **Favorites** in the sidebar to see only your starred games. Search and platform filters still work there.
- Favorites are remembered locally after closing the application. The star works with a mouse or keyboard and does not open or modify a save.
- Short fades between library and editor and gentle cover highlights keep navigation responsive. Covers retain their full artwork and size.
- Enable **Reduce motion** in the sidebar to stop animations immediately; your preference is remembered.
- Pending edits, focus, and library scroll position are preserved while navigating or managing favorites.

All 112 game-editor and interface tests pass. The portable Windows executable is checked for startup and its bundled resources.

Saving continues to update the opened file directly with the same name and without automatic backups. Game compatibility and experimental labels are unchanged.

Download UniversalSaveEditor.exe and open it with a double click.
